Nishitachi Night with a Local: Wagyu, Shochu and a Bar

### Short description (159 chars)

wagyu plate, shochu tasting and a bar the regulars go to.

### Highlights
- Meet an English-speaking Miyazaki local; walk into places you would not find alone
- One plate of Miyazaki wagyu and one drink to start
- Shochu tasting the way it is poured in the south
- A local bar where the regulars are — reserved seats, no line
- Small groups only: two to six guests, twice a week
- Everything within a five-minute walk in Nishitachi

### Full description
Nishitachi is where Miyazaki drinks. Two or three streets of tiny bars and
counter kitchens, packed between office blocks in the middle of the city. It
is not on any itinerary and not in any guidebook — the doors are small, the
menus are in Japanese, and the regulars all know each other. This is the
evening we take you into it.

You meet your host at a landmark in central Miyazaki City at 19:00. From
there it is a short walk to a counter kitchen where the first plate is
Miyazaki wagyu — the beef this prefecture is known for — with one drink.
Around 20:15 you move on to a small shochu bar. Southern Kyushu is the home
of shochu, and this is where you taste it the way it is poured here, not
the way it is sold abroad. By 21:15 you are at a local bar. Reserved seats,
regulars around you, and a host who can translate what they are saying if
you want to talk. At 22:00 we say goodnight at the same landmark where we
met.

The evening is designed for two to six guests. Twice a week only. Everything
is within a five-minute walk. Guests must be 20 or older (Japan's legal
drinking age). Please arrange your own transport to and from central
Miyazaki City.

Price: ¥25,000 per guest. Includes one wagyu plate, shochu tasting and two
drinks. Additional food and drinks are not included. Free cancellation up
to 24 hours before start time.
